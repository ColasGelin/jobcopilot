## Write
- `cv_tweaks`: 2-3 concrete things to emphasise or reword on the CV for this job.
- `cover_note`: at most 120 words.
- `outreach_message`: a LinkedIn connection note the candidate sends after applying, to the person in `contact_hint`:
  say they applied for this role and give one concrete reason they fit. At most 280 characters (LinkedIn cuts notes
  at 300): a short greeting, the message, the candidate's first name.
- `contact_hint`: who to send it to and why: the person named in the posting if there is one, otherwise the role most
  likely to read it (the recruiter for this team, the engineering lead, or the founder at a small company).
- `contact_search`: the words to type into LinkedIn's people search to find that person: their name and the company
  when the posting names them, otherwise the company and the role (e.g. "Acme talent acquisition").

Rules: write in the same language as the posting (English, Spanish or French). Be specific to this company and role.
Plain, human tone; no clichés like "I am excited to apply" or "passionate". Never invent experience, skills or
numbers that are not in the candidate profile.
